## Executive Overview

**[LJOS.io](https://ljos.io)** (formerly LawnJobOS.io) merges content publishing, local search discovery, and instant appointment scheduling into a unified workspace for independent service business owners (auto detailers, fitness coaches, cleaners, handymen, wellness studios, lawn care professionals).

- **Core Slogan**: POST. SHARE. GET BOOKED.
- **Official System**: **LJOS Software** & **LJOS AI Marketing Tools**
- **Pricing Model**: **100% Free Forever** base software cost • $0 upfront (No credit card required to build) • 7% fee per completed job via Stripe payouts.
- **Setup Speed**: Get Bookable in 60 Seconds

---

## Technical Specifications & Features

### 1. Social Booking Engine™ (Post-to-Job Linking)
* Embed direct calendar scheduling and checkout triggers directly into portfolio posts and image/video updates.
* Allows clients to transition seamlessly from viewing social content to securing a booked appointment.

### 2. Upfront Card Checkout & Deposit Engine
* Integrated Stripe card processing supporting 1-click booking checkouts, dynamic quote requests, and automated deposit holds.
* Pay-per-visit interval scheduling (Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Custom) with zero upfront subscription barriers.

### 3. Unified Operator Workspace
* Replaces fragmented website builders, external scheduling widgets, and separate invoice apps with one centralized dashboard.
* Eliminates software overhead so service operators pay $0 during off-seasons, vacations, or slow months.
