# LJOS.io — Social Booking Platform™

**Your work is already social. Now make it bookable.**

LJOS.io connects service businesses and customers through Business Pages, social content, online booking, quotes and messaging.

This repository provides a public overview of LJOS platform features and workflows. It is not an API reference or a complete specification of the production system.

## What is social booking?

Social booking connects shared work and customer recommendations with a practical next step: exploring a business, discussing a service or making a booking.

On LJOS, businesses can tag services in posts and share their content through their Business Page, the LJOS Social Feed and external social channels.

Customers can explore shared work, recommend businesses and return to their accounts to manage bookings and conversations.

**Post. Share. Booked.**

## For service businesses

### LJOS Business Page

Create a dedicated page to present your business, showcase work and offer services.

Customers can explore public service pages without signing in. Signing in is required to message, follow or book.

### Bookable social content

Share work and updates with services attached, giving customers a direct route from a post to the relevant service.

### Services, quotes and booking

Offer preselected services or discuss customer requirements through quotes and messaging.

Quotes can be refined through ongoing conversations, including images and availability discussions. Once a quote is accepted and a date selected by the customer or operator, it creates a calendar event.

### Scheduling and recurring services

Manage appointments and offer recurring services where enabled.

Customers can manage bookings from their dashboards. Business-initiated appointment changes use a permission request sent to the customer with a reason attached.

### Connected messaging

Messaging is incorporated throughout the platform to keep service discussions, quotes and booking communication connected.

### MyStore

Create a product catalogue with images, descriptions, prices and available quantities.

Products belong to the individual business and appear through its Business Page. Relevant products can also appear through LJOSAI Suggest during booking.

### Payments

Service payment timing depends on the operator's settings. Services can also offer a fixed-amount or percentage deposit where enabled.

For payment on completion, the operator must clear the completed job to initiate payment processing through Stripe.

Payout timing depends on the payment provider, account status and banking schedules.

## For customers

Customers can discover businesses through invitations, the LJOS Social Feed, recommendations and shared content on other social channels.

With an LJOS account, customers can:

- Book services or discuss requirements with a business.
- Exchange messages and refine quotes.
- Manage appointments and recurring bookings.
- Revisit past services and rebook.
- Personalise a Customer Page.
- Share posts through My Posts.
- Recommend businesses and share their public page.

Notifications are available in the dashboard, with email and SMS notifications where enabled.

## Platform fees

LJOS has no monthly subscription or annual platform fee.

- The LJOS platform fee is 7% of the service price on completed paid jobs.
- Stripe processing fees are separate.
- Customers pay a $2.50 booking fee.

### Planned monthly cap: No Growth Tax

LJOS is integrating a monthly cap on the 7% platform fee.

Under the planned model, once the cap is reached, the 7% platform fee stops for the remainder of that monthly period. Stripe processing fees and the customer-facing $2.50 booking fee continue.

The cap amount, launch date and detailed rules have not yet been confirmed. This is a planned change, not a claim that the cap is currently active.
